# Pal’Alma


## The Indiana Jones of mezcal
Mezcalero: **Erick Rodriguez**, dubbed as the the *Indiana Jones of mezcal*. widely known as one of Mexico's most knowledgable and adventerous bottlers of Mezcal.[2](https://shop.klwines.com/products/details/1485032). 


As an adventureure: Anywhere he finds agave fields and a freshwater stream, there’s a good chance that someone is making mezcal nearby. [4](https://www.mezcalreviews.com/filter-by/brand/mezcalito-palalma/)

## Brand

Almamezcalera is his flagship brand and Pal’Alma is the export brand. [1](https://www.mezcalistas.com/brand/almamezcalera/). 

Bottled as aguardiente de agave, and are rare to found outside of Mexico. Some(limited) release can be occasionally found under label *Wahaka VdM*[5](https://www.mezcalreviews.com/wahaka-vdm-at-the-pastry-war/). 

> Wahaka is a mezcal brand itself, ([link](https://www.wahakamezcal.com/)), Wahaka VdM(VdM = Vinos de México) is a line that collects hardest to find mezcals in the market, which usually the most expensive

## Mode
Erick Rodriguez collaborates with a single mezcalero per state, inside and outside DO. He bottles in tiny batches.

## Tasting room


A post by team MezcalReviews[7](https://www.mezcalreviews.com/almamezcalera-mezcalito-palalma/) says: In his tasting room, among the highlights was a mezcal made from a 55 year-old Karwinskii agave
and till 2017, he has walked through 20+ states finding the mezcals.

Appointment-only. “I only take aficionados that are truly passionate.”[6](https://www.liquor.com/articles/erick-rodriguez/)
