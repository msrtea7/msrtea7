## Ocho

2008, [Highlands]

Team up: Carlos Camarena(5th gen agave farmer, also master distiller) teamed up with Tomas Estes(important man bringing tquila culture to Europe, also a bar & restaurant owner)

Origin of 8:
- Made from the 8th sample created by the Camarenas for Tomas.
- 8 years for the agaves to ripen before being harvested.
- Founded in 2008
- 8 days from when the agaves reach the distillery until it becomes blanco tequila.
- 8 kG of agave to make 1L of Ocho tequila
- Carlos Camarena is one of 8 siblings
- 8 decadeS of The Camarenas FAMILY producing tequila.
- Ocho Reposado is aged for 8 weeks and 8 days.
