## Introduction

An AI assistant for independent bars that manages business operations such as inventory management and POS systems through natural conversation.

Ask any question about your bar data and get instant answers:

- **Natural Language Interface**: Conversational format like chatGPT
- **Easy record**: Read, write, delete, update the database using natual language.
- **Customizable Knowledge**: Record your cocktail recipes, seasonal menus, and supplier relationships
- **Actionable Insights**: Provides data calculation and simple insights with or without pre-defined formula. And it goes along thought processes or specific recommendations (if needed), replacing routine accounting work

My vision for the Barbot is: Let bartenders do what bartenders do.

## Limitations

The biggest challenge is data acquisition. Every establishment has different POS systems - some use Meituan, some use Yinbao, others have custom systems, requiring individual API adaptations. For Excel-based data, formats vary wildly, making data quality and complexity difficult to assess. Real-time inventory updates are also challenging and require continuous updating.

For example, the simple simulated system I've currently completed might require:
- Complete database design
- 1-3 months of sales records and purchase orders
- Complete cocktail recipe lists and supplier information

The ideas and technical approaches I'm implementing are rapidly evolving in industry. If such a system is meaningful, investing some time to digitize bar assets might reveal more possibilities.
